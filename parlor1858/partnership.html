How it works

Four steps to launch.

  1. Pick your option. Check a box below and sign. That is the whole agreement.
  2. We install. MCTV mounts and connects the screen in the Parlor. You supply wall space and a standard outlet.
  3. We build your ad. Send us your logo, photos, and current specials. Our team designs a 30-second HD spot for your approval.
  4. You go live. Your ad enters the Oxford loop. Options 2 and 3 add 15 or 20 more screens; Option 3 also puts MCTV to work on your Google Business Profile.
