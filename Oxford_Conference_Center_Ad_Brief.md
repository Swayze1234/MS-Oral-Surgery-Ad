# Oxford Conference Center — 30-Second Silent Digital Ad
Design instructions (paste into Canva / Adobe Express / CapCut, or hand to a designer)

## Global settings
- Canvas: 1920 x 1080 (16:9). Duplicate to 1080 x 1920 if a vertical version is needed.
- Length: 30 seconds exactly. No audio track. No music.
- Look: minimal, photo-first. Photos do the work; text stays small and quiet.
- Every photo frame: full-bleed photo with a **white gradient** on the left 45% (100% white at the left edge fading to 0% at about the center). Text sits inside the white zone.
- Motion on photos: slow zoom-in (scale 100% to 106%) for the full length of each frame. Nothing else moves.
- Transition between frames: 0.5-second cross-dissolve.
- Text motion: simple fade-in only (0.6 seconds). No slides, bounces, or typewriter effects.
- Logo files: use the **navy version** everywhere (it sits on white). The white version is only for a dark background and is not needed in this ad.
- Fonts (match the logo): headlines in a classic serif like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond, body and labels in a clean sans-serif like Montserrat with wide letter-spacing.
- Colors from the logo: headline navy `#2C4A5A`, accent gold `#B89B5E`. Use gold for the small labels, sub-lines, and the Frame 4 tagline. No other colors.
- Safe margins: keep all text at least 100 px from every edge.

## Timeline

### Frame 1 — 0:00 to 0:07
- Photo: **speaker at the "OXFORD CONFERENCE CENTER" podium with a full room** (round tables, red banners behind). Crop so the podium and crowd fill the right two-thirds; the branded podium should stay readable.
- White gradient: left side.
- Text (fades in at 0:00.5):
  - Headline, bold, ~72 px, 2 lines: `Want record attendance at your next conference?`
  - Sub-line, regular, ~40 px, accent color, fades in at 0:02: `Pick a city people love.`

### Frame 2 — 0:07 to 0:15
- Two photos, one cut, text stays put:
  - 0:07 to 0:11 — **building exterior** (white columns, red brick, flowers). Slow zoom toward the front doors.
  - 0:11 to 0:15 — **wide empty ballroom** (round tables, wood doors, projectors). Half-second cross-dissolve between the two.
- White gradient: left side on both photos.
- Text (fades in at 0:07.5):
  - Small label, ~28 px, accent color, letter-spaced: `OXFORD, MISSISSIPPI`
  - Headline, bold, ~60 px: `The Square. Ole Miss.` then new line `25,000+ sq ft of newly renovated meeting space.`

### Frame 3 — 0:15 to 0:24
- Photo: **Women's Summit stage** (bright red backdrop, cream armchairs, black tables with white flowers). Crop so the red stage sits on the right; the color is the eye-catcher of the whole ad.
- White gradient: left side.
- Text: four short lines that fade in one after another, 0.8 seconds apart, starting at 0:15.5. Bold, ~48 px, stacked:
  - `300+ capacity`
  - `Hotels next door`
  - `Free parking`
  - `Full catering options`
- Then at 0:20 a closing line fades in below, regular, ~40 px, accent color: `One call plans it all.`

### Frame 4 — 0:24 to 0:30 (logo animation, 5 seconds)
- Photo: **empty auditorium** (curved ceiling, rows of seats, OXFORD podium and screen). Keep it very faded: white gradient covers the whole frame at about 85% opacity so the room is only a hint.
- Logo animation (0:24 to 0:28.5), built from the logo's own three pieces, centered. Logo width about 900 px on a 1920 canvas:
  - 0:24.0 — The gold diamond fades in at center, then the gold line **draws outward** from the diamond to both sides over 0.8 seconds.
  - 0:24.8 — `OXFORD` (navy serif) fades in above the line while rising 20 px into place, 0.8 seconds.
  - 0:25.4 — `CONFERENCE CENTER` (navy sans) fades in below the line, 0.6 seconds. Logo is now complete.
  - 0:26.4 — Tagline fades in under the logo, serif italic, ~44 px, gold: `Elevate Your Event`
  - 0:27.4 — Contact line fades in, sans, ~30 px, navy, letter-spaced: `662.232.3367  ·  oxfordconferencecenter.com`
  - 0:28.5 to 0:30 — hold everything still.
  - If the tool can't split the logo into pieces, just fade the whole navy logo in while scaling 92% to 100% over 1.2 seconds, then continue from the tagline step.
- End on the still frame (no fade to black).

## Photo assignment cheat sheet
| Frame | Photo | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Speaker at OCC podium, full room | Shows attendance and the brand |
| 2a | Building exterior | Puts you in Oxford |
| 2b | Wide empty ballroom | Shows the size of the space |
| 3 | Women's Summit red stage | Color pop, polished production |
| 4 | Empty auditorium (faded) | Quiet backdrop for the logo |

Spares, in order of usefulness: gala table close-up (swap into Frame 3 if the red stage feels too busy), auditorium with audience (backup for Frame 1), boardroom, ballroom with screens, gala room with flags.

## Quick checks before export
- Play it muted at full screen: every line should be readable in under 2 seconds.
- Total text on screen at any moment: never more than 3 lines except Frame 3.
- Export: MP4, H.264, 30 fps, no audio. Also export a PNG of Frame 4 as the static fallback.
- Logo clear space: keep other text at least one "diamond width" away from the logo on all sides.
