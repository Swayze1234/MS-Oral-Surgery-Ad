Over the last fifteen months your ad ran 330,221 times across 25 active venues, adding up to 1,834 hours and 33 minutes of screen time. Those are real hours in front of real people — neighbors, patients, students, and families moving through the places they already visit every week in Starkville and the Golden Triangle.

Starkville carried the campaign with 23 venues and 92.5% of total plays, while Elm Lake Golf Course in Columbus contributed another 7.4% on its own. Your strongest hosts were Uno Mas Tacos y Tequila (80,272 plays), Two Brothers Smoked Meats (28,280), and your own pharmacy screen (24,915) — proof that high-dwell restaurants and everyday service stops do the heaviest lifting. General locations, restaurants and bars, and medical and dental offices together produced 77.7% of your exposure, which means the message is landing both during downtime and in health-minded moments.

One number worth sitting with: your reach was never concentrated in a handful of screens. The twenty venues outside your top five still generated 154,203 plays — 46.7% of the campaign — and an estimated 286,000 monthly impressions. That breadth is what makes a local name feel like it's everywhere, and it's the part of this campaign that Section 05 is about.

Every play above ran at no cost to BJ's. The host partnership includes five free screens; the other twenty venues were gifted while network inventory was open — roughly $500/month of advertising at standard rates, $7,500+ across the campaign.

The network is filling up

When this campaign started, screen loops had room to spare, so every host partner ran network-wide at no charge. That's no longer the case: paid advertisers are signing on and loop inventory is being committed to their agreements. As of this fall, every host partner on the network moves to the standard host benefit — five free screens — and the remaining slots are allocated to signed campaigns, first come, first served.

What five screens keeps — and what it doesn't

Five well-chosen screens are genuinely valuable; that's why the host benefit exists. But your last fifteen months show how much of your presence lives beyond them. Even if you keep your five best performers, the twenty screens you'd give up carried 46.7% of your plays and an estimated 286,000 monthly impressions — more than half your current reach, including every screen in Columbus and West Point but one.
