WHAT HOST PARTNERS GET

Hosting an MCTV screen makes The Warehouse Market more than a venue — it makes you a partner. Every host partner receives all of the following, completely free, for as long as you host:

5 Free Advertising Screens
Your ad running in five venues of your choice across the network — restaurants, clinics, salons, shops — at no cost, every month.
Custom HD Ad Production
A custom 15–30 second high-definition spot showcasing your deals, location, and team — designed and built by our in-house creative team.
Quarterly Creative Refreshes
A fresh ad build every quarter, free — holiday promos, seasonal items, new arrivals, whatever's moving that month.
Unlimited Rotation
Your spot runs on a 15-minute loop — 1,500+ plays per screen every month. Unskippable, unblockable, impossible to scroll past.
Proof-of-Play Reporting
Real numbers, venue by venue — exactly where and how often your ad ran, sourced from NTV360 network analytics.
You Own Your Ad Forever
Every spot we produce for you is yours to keep — use it on social media, your website, anywhere you like.
The network you're part of
