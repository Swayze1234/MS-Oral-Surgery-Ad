What Host Partners Get

Everything below. Free.

Hosting an MCTV screen would make The Warehouse Market more than a venue — it makes you a partner in the fastest-growing owner-operated indoor billboard network in North America, with screens across Oxford, Tupelo, and the Golden Triangle. Every host partner receives all of the following at no cost, for as long as you host:

5 Free Advertising Screens
Your ad in five venues of your choice across the network — restaurants, clinics, salons, shops — free every month.
Custom HD Ad Production
A 15–30 second high-definition spot showcasing your deals, location, and team — built by our in-house creative team.
Quarterly Creative Refreshes
A fresh ad build every quarter — holiday promos, seasonal items, new arrivals, whatever's moving that month.
Unlimited Rotation
A 15-minute loop delivering 1,500+ plays per screen monthly. Unskippable, unblockable, impossible to scroll past.
Proof-of-Play Reporting
Real numbers, venue by venue — exactly where and how often your ad ran, from NTV360 network analytics.
You Own Your Ad Forever
Every spot we produce is yours to keep — use it on social media, your website, anywhere you like.
