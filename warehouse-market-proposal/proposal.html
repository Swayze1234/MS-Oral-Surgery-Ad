WHAT'S CHANGING THIS FALL

5 Free
Every host venue partner earns five screen locations at no cost. While inventory was open, we went further and ran host ads network-wide free — The Warehouse Market has been reaching customers all over town without spending a dollar.
The network is filling up

Paid advertisers are signing on and loop inventory is being committed to their agreements. As of this fall, every host partner on the network moves to the standard host benefit — five free screens — and the remaining slots are allocated to signed campaigns, first come, first served. Before any of those screens is released to a paying advertiser, we're giving our host partners first right to keep them at a rate no advertiser can access.

Why keep the bigger footprint

Our screens average 1,500+ plays per screen every month in venues where people wait — 55+ minutes of average dwell time, with no skipping, blocking, or scrolling past. A five-screen presence keeps your name in five rooms; a fifteen- or twenty-five-screen footprint makes The Warehouse Market feel like it's everywhere your customers already go. That's how a local name becomes the first name people think of.
