The agreement

The terms, in plain words.

TERM
Six-month minimum. After that, month to month, with 30 days' written notice to stop. Prepaid terms run seven or fourteen months on air.
BILLING
On signing, the first month is billed, or the full amount if you pay up front. On monthly billing, the next cycle starts 30 days after your ads are approved and live.
CREATIVE
MCTV designs and produces both spots: Little & Holland's :15 and the rental car :30. Silent, with on-screen text. Three rounds of changes each, then $200 a round. A fresh spot each quarter if you want one. You own them.
SCREENS
Little & Holland picks the option; the rental car spot runs on the same screens. Host venues are independent businesses, and MCTV may substitute a venue. Proof of play every month.
SPONSORSHIP
Option 4 only. Presenting sponsor of MCTV Traffic on the Oxford board, credited to the business you pick on page eight.
BAR FILING
Little & Holland's spot, and its traffic lockup if the firm is the sponsor, is filed with the Mississippi Bar before it first airs: $25 per ad, paid by the firm. We hand you the Bar Filing Packet: the file as it will run, a transcript and a where-and-when schedule. The rental car spot needs no filing.
ONE CONTACT
One agreement, one invoice, one point of contact: Swayze Hollingsworth.
