# SERVPRO creative: brand compliance checklist (from the Brand Identity Guide, Jan 2026)

Applies to both pieces: the 26-second sponsored schedule (intro, board, outro) and the 30-second spot.

## Fonts
- Use **Red Hat Display** (headlines, franchise name) and **Red Hat Text** (body). Red Hat is the brand-sanctioned Google font for franchises without Adobe CC. Graphie is the primary brand font but is Adobe-licensed; Arial is the last-resort fallback. Fonts are in `servpro/fonts/`.
- Replace every other typeface in the SERVPRO portions of both spots. The MCTV schedule styling (the condensed italic headers) is MCTV's design and is fine where it is not SERVPRO copy.

## Logo
- Use the approved **house (roof) logo** from the brand kit. Orange **#FF6720** (RGB 255 103 32) only. Do not recolor, outline, tint, or reduce opacity. The white knockout version is only for when the orange logo lacks contrast; a black knockout is not recommended.
- Franchise name reads exactly **SERVPRO® of Columbus / Starkville** (SERVPRO in caps with ®, "of" lowercase, the name as written in the franchise license).

## Required copy (video advertising)
- **Independently owned and operated** must appear in all television and video advertising. No period. Small is fine, but it has to be on screen (end card and sponsor panel).
- Tagline is **Like it never even happened.®** in sentence case with the ®. Not all caps.
- Brand descriptor **Cleaning. Restoration. Construction.** when there is room (the guide says use the descriptor instead of the tagline where a tagline already appears elsewhere; on the panel both fit, on a tight end card keep the tagline).
- One phone number: **662-324-3003**. 1-800-SERVPRO is only required where TV cameras or a seated stadium audience can see the graphic. Indoor venue screens do not trigger that rule.

## Colors
- SERVPRO Orange **#FF6720** and SERVPRO Green **#6CC24A** only. White, orange, green or light text on dark backgrounds is preferred.
