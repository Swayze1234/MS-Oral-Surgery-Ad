Financial Concepts is one of the Golden Triangle's most established names in personal finance — a family-owned, independent advisory firm founded by Rhonda Ferguson in 1988 and led today by her son, CEO & Principal Planner Scott Ferguson. From its Columbus office on North Frontage Road, the team manages roughly $1.1 billion for families, business owners, professionals, and retirees across the region.

The firm's practice covers the full arc of a client's financial life: retirement and college planning, portfolio management, retirement-plan distribution, long-term care, and legacy planning for heirs. Its promise — planning that goes beyond the numbers to the family, health, and dreams behind them — is exactly the kind of message that rewards repetition in front of the right audience.
