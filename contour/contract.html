Terms, in plain English

  1. 1What you get. MCTV runs your :15 twice in every loop at every Oxford location on page 2 during Flight 1, at the 26 marked locations during Flight 2, and holds the title sponsor position on the Tupelo Regional flight board from the first play through March 12, 2027. This order is for Oxford and the board. The Tupelo venue screens, Starkville and the Golden Triangle are not in it.
  2. 2The flights. Flight 1 runs from the first play through December 5, 2026. If the first play lands after October 10, 2026, every missed day is added to the front of Flight 2, so the two flights together are never shorter than sixteen weeks. Flight 2 runs January 18 through March 12, 2027 on the 26 locations marked on page 2. Nothing runs in Oxford, and nothing is billed for Oxford, from December 6 through January 17.
  3. 3Flight 2 locations. The 26 are chosen for rooms that skew higher income and more discretionary spending. Contour may swap any of them for another location of the same kind on the Flight 1 list by writing to MCTV by January 4, 2027. MCTV may add locations to Flight 2 at no charge but will not remove one without Contour's okay.
  4. 4Play floor. Every Oxford screen in the running flight delivers at least 1,500 plays of your spot in each full calendar month it runs. A partial month is prorated by days on air. Days lost waiting on creative approval do not count against the floor. If a screen falls short, MCTV makes up the missing plays in the next month of the same flight, or extends that screen day for day. That is the only remedy for missed plays or downtime, and MCTV's total liability under this order is limited to what Contour has paid.
  5. 5Creative. MCTV designs and produces the spot and the board sponsor position at no extra cost and refreshes the spot for Flight 2. You approve both before they air. Ads on this network are silent. Creative on the terminal screens is also subject to the Tupelo Airport Authority's content approval under its venue agreement.
  6. 6The flight board. MCTV maintains the board. Contour agrees to add MCTV to its schedule distribution so changes reach the board the day they are published. If a terminal screen is down, moved or covered during the terminal renovation, the title continues on the remaining screens and MCTV extends the board term day for day at no charge.
  7. 7Traction reports. Each month MCTV sends a traction report for your spot: plays and air time by location, with the per-screen play log behind it. Plays are counted by the player, not viewers. MCTV does not report audience impressions for the screens.
  8. 8Your content, your rights. You hold the rights to anything you supply. MCTV cannot clear collegiate or other licensed marks for you.
  9. 9Host venues. Host venues are independent businesses. If one closes or leaves the network, MCTV substitutes a location of the same kind in Oxford when one is available, and tells you.
  10. 10Payment and invoicing. $4,250 is due at signing and is invoiced by email the day this order is countersigned. The first play starts once it is paid, or once you tell us a check is in the mail. $3,000 is invoiced by email by January 1, 2027 and due February 1, 2027. Every invoice goes to Accountspayable@flycontour.com and shows the order number and Contour's PO. Flight 2 and the rest of the board term are part of this order. If the February payment is late, Flight 2 and the board title pause until it is paid. The payment is still owed, paused days are not made up, and the play floor does not apply while paused. A $30 fee applies to a returned or NSF payment, and is invoiced.
  11. 11Fall 2027 renewal option. Contour may renew the Oxford fall flight for fall 2027, eight weeks on the 40 Oxford locations on page 2 with the same rotation, floor and reporting, at $4,250, by telling MCTV in writing by July 1, 2027. The board title is not part of the renewal unless both sides agree in writing. The renewal is a new order, paid at signing. There is no obligation to renew.
  12. 12Binding. This order is three pages. It becomes binding when both sides sign and takes effect on the date MCTV countersigns. Scans, PDFs and e-signatures count as originals. This order is the whole agreement and replaces order MCTV-20261001-CNTR, the one-pager, The Season and any earlier proposal. Governed by the laws of the State of Mississippi.
