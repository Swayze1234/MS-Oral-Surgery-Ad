Terms in plain English

  1. 1.What you get. MCTV runs Contour's :15 twice in every loop at every Schedule A location for Flight 1 and holds the title sponsor position on the Tupelo Regional flight board from the first play through January 17, 2027. The spot and the board are two separate placements.
  2. 2.Term and price. $4,250, one invoice due on receipt, for eight weeks on air in Oxford, first play through December 5, 2026, plus the board title through January 17, 2027. Time on air starts on the first play date above. If the first play lands after October 10, every missed day is carried as a day-for-day credit on the Oxford screens toward Contour's next MCTV order. Ends when the term ends. Does not renew on its own.
  3. 3.Exclusivity. While this order runs, MCTV places no other airline, competing airport, airport authority or destination marketing organization on the Oxford screens or in the board sponsor position.
  4. 4.The flight board. MCTV maintains the board; Contour adds MCTV to its schedule distribution so changes post the day they are published. If a terminal screen is down, moved or covered during the renovation, the title continues on the remaining screens and the board term extends day for day at no charge. Terminal creative is also subject to Tupelo Airport Authority content approval.
  5. 5.Play floor and make-good. If an Oxford screen falls short of its prorated floor in a month, MCTV makes up the missing plays the next month or extends that screen day for day. Days lost waiting on creative approval do not count against the floor. That is the only remedy for missed plays or downtime, and MCTV's total liability under this order is limited to what Contour has paid.
  6. 6.Creative. MCTV designs and produces the spot and the board position at no additional charge. Contour approves both before they air. Ads on this network are silent.
  7. 7.Brand rights. Contour holds the rights to anything it supplies and confirms it may use the Contour name, logos and photography in local advertising. MCTV cannot clear collegiate or other licensed marks for Contour.
  8. 8.Locations. Host venues are independent businesses and the list can change. If one closes or leaves the network, MCTV substitutes a location of the same kind in Oxford when one is available, and tells Contour. The traction report shows every location the spot ran.
  9. 9.Measurement. Plays are counted by the media player, not viewers. MCTV does not report audience impressions for the screens and makes no impression claims under this order.
  10. 10.Invoicing. After Contour signs, MCTV emails one QuickBooks invoice for $4,250 to Accountspayable@flycontour.com, attention Chris Hanvy, showing the order number and Contour's PO. Nothing is invoiced by mail only. MCTV's W-9 and signed ACH letter accompany the invoice.
  11. 11.Payment. The invoice is due on receipt and before the first play; the first play starts once it is paid, or once Contour says a check is in the mail. A $30 fee applies to returned or NSF transactions. Prepaid order for the full term; if ended early, time paid for is not refunded. If MCTV cannot deliver the campaign, the only remedy is a refund of what was paid, in proportion to the time on air not delivered.
  12. 12.Fall 2027 option. Contour may run the Oxford fall flight again for fall 2027, eight weeks on the Schedule A locations with the same rotation, floor and reporting, at $4,250, by telling MCTV in writing by July 1, 2027. The board title is not part of that option unless both sides agree in writing. No obligation to renew.
