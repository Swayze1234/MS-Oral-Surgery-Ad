## Why they're a strong MCTV prospect

1. **They're the "new bank in town" in Northeast Mississippi.** In October 2023
   Guaranty completed its acquisition of Iuka-based First American National Bank
   (~$384M in assets, 11 branches across Corinth, Iuka, Burnsville, Booneville,
   Tishomingo, Belmont, Saltillo, Tupelo, Fulton, and Amory). Those branches now
   carry the Guaranty name — a Delta brand that most Tupelo-area consumers didn't
   grow up with. They have a genuine, ongoing brand-awareness problem in exactly
   the market MCTV covers, competing against entrenched hometown names like
   Renasant and Cadence (both Tupelo-rooted).

2. **Their footprint maps almost exactly onto MCTV's three markets.**
   - **Tupelo:** two branches — Downtown (431 W Main St, Ste 201) and North
     Gloster (4289 N Gloster St) — plus nearby Saltillo, Fulton, Amory, and the
     whole Corinth/Booneville corridor.
   - **Oxford:** University Branch, 1500 University Ave.
   - **Golden Triangle:** Starkville branch, 100 Mill St.
   One MCTV network buy reaches all three of their growth markets at once.

3. **They already spend on local visibility in Tupelo.** They are a Community
   Development Foundation (CDF) member, presenting sponsor of the Tupelo Young
   Professionals **Community Crawfish Boil** (5th annual), and sponsored the TYP
   Community Q&A (Aug 2025). A community bank that sponsors crawfish boils and
   chamber events is a natural fit for screens in local restaurants, gyms, and
   waiting rooms — same audience, always-on.

## Suggested pitch angles

- **"Renasant and Cadence own the signage in this town — you're the challenger."**
  MCTV puts the Guaranty name in front of Tupelo consumers daily at a fraction of
  broadcast/billboard cost, exactly where banking decisions get talked about.
- **Single buy, whole footprint:** Oxford + Tupelo + Golden Triangle screens match
  their University, Lee County, and Starkville branches — no wasted impressions
  in markets they don't serve.
- **Community-bank story fits the medium:** their marketing leans on local
  sponsorships and "locally owned since 1943." Venue-based screens in locally
  owned businesses reinforce that positioning.
- **Timing:** they're still converting First American customers and building name
  recognition in NE Mississippi — awareness spend now has a clear business case
  (deposit retention + new-household acquisition).

## Watch-outs

- Decision-making may route through Belzoni (HQ) even for Tupelo-market spend —
  get John Curlee bought in first, then let him carry it up.
- Bank marketing is compliance-conscious; creative approvals may take longer
  than a typical local advertiser.
- Don't confuse them with **Guaranty Bancshares (Texas)** — a different,
  similarly named institution that dominates search results.
