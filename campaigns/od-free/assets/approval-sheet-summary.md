# Approval sheet summary (from Creed's PDF, Sep 30)

Source: `MCTV_OD-Free_MSDH_Spots-for-approval_2026-09-30.pdf`. Text only; the
PDF itself lives in `spots/` once added.

## The pilot
- **Where:** 75+ MCTV screens in Oxford venues
- **How often:** 4 plays per hour, every screen, all open hours
- **How long:** 30 days from go-live
- **Cost:** no charge, no contract
- **Creative:** three :15 spots and three :10 spots in rotation, plus a still of each as fallback
- **Start:** the day after MSDH approves the creative
- **After the pilot:** same creative can run statewide (Oxford, Tupelo, Starkville, Columbus, Jackson metro, Hattiesburg and Laurel, Gulf Coast). Nothing is Oxford-specific.

## How every spot is built
Message on the left. On the right for every second: MSDH logo, FREE NALOXONE
KIT, the QR code (451 px, scannable across a room), SCAN TO REQUEST, and the
OD Free wordmark. Colors: OD Free red, yellow, orange; MSDH navy and gold.
Every health line is MSDH's or OD Free's wording. The QR opens MSDH's
Naloxone (Narcan) Kit Request Form.
