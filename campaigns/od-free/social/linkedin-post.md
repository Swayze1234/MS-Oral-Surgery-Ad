```
MCTV Digital is proud to partner with the Mississippi State Department of Health on OD Free Mississippi.

Starting today, every MCTV screen across North Mississippi (Oxford, Tupelo and the Golden Triangle) is carrying one message: free naloxone is available to every Mississippi resident, and the QR code on the screen takes you straight to MSDH's request form.
```
