## Comment moderation

This topic draws strong reactions. Plan for it:

- **Reply once** to questions with the link. Don't debate.
- **Hide** comments that sell or ask for pills or drugs, and report them. Hide comments naming victims.
- **Leave up** criticism that isn't abusive. Hiding it reads badly on a public-health post.
- Someone in crisis in the comments: reply with the 988 line and the link, and message them directly.
- Turn on Meta's profanity filter and keyword block for drug-sale terms before posting (Page settings → Moderation).
